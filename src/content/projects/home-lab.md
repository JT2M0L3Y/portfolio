I built a virtualization lab on Proxmox and Debian to explore infrastructure and self-hosted services. The setup includes isolated networks and uses Tailscale for zero-trust access, AdGuard for DNS filtering, Vault for secrets management, and Caddy for HTTPS.

This is personal project experience rather than production infrastructure.
